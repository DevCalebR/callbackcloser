# CallbackCloser — Product Brief

## One-line description
CallbackCloser helps local businesses turn missed calls into booked jobs automatically.

## Problem
Local businesses miss calls while working, driving, serving customers, or after hours. Every missed call can become a lost lead, lost booking, or lost job.

## Target customers
- Plumbers
- HVAC companies
- Roofers
- Electricians
- Cleaning companies
- Landscapers
- Auto detailers
- Barbers and salons
- Med spas
- Small service businesses

## Core workflow
1. A customer calls the business.
2. The business misses the call.
3. CallbackCloser automatically sends a text message to the caller.
4. The customer replies with what they need.
5. The business owner receives the lead.
6. The lead is saved in the dashboard.
7. The business follows up and marks the lead as contacted, booked, or lost.

## Main value proposition
CallbackCloser helps businesses recover missed-call leads before they choose a competitor.

## MVP features
- Twilio phone number or call forwarding
- Missed-call detection
- Automatic SMS follow-up
- Custom business reply message
- Lead inbox/dashboard
- Owner notifications
- Lead status tracking
- Basic business settings

## Future features

- AI lead summaries
- Appointment booking
